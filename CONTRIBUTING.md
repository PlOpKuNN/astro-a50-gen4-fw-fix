# Contributing

Useful contributions are logs and reproducible reports from other A50 Gen 4 units.

When reporting a result, include:

- A50 variant (Xbox/PC or PlayStation/PC)
- ACC version
- base firmware version
- headset firmware version
- whether RX_MCU previously completed successfully
- the relevant ACC log section from `starting for RX_MCU` through the failure/finish

Please do not upload proprietary ASTRO/Logitech executables, DLLs or firmware blobs to issues or pull requests.
